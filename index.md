## About Me

I am a first-year PhD student in Linguistics at Boston University. Before that, I received a B.A. from Beijing Normal University in Beijing, China and a M.A. from Zhejiang University in Hangzhou, China. I am particularly interested in studying humanities through rigorous and systematic scientific inquiry.

## Research Interests

**Broad:** Formal semantics, Generative syntax  
**Narrow:** Degree semantics, adjectives, numerals, *wh*-indefinites, incrementality
